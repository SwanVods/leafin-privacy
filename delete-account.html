Delete Your Account and Data

Applies to: Leafin (Android app by Bandulan Studio)  ·  Last updated: August 27, 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Leafin account and the personal data associated with it, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete your account from within the app

If you still have the app installed and can sign in, this is the fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open the Leafin app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Help.
  3. Tap Delete My Data.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately upon confirmation. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, can no longer sign in, or prefer to request deletion directly, email us at ariefgraifhan@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within a reasonable time, and in any case no later than 30 days from a verified request.

What gets deleted, and what is retained

Data typeOutcome
Account profile (name, email, profile photo)Permanently deleted
Transactions, wallets, budgets, and categoriesPermanently deleted
Notification history and push subscription dataPermanently deleted
Subscription/purchase recordsRetained only as required for financial record-keeping, tax, and legal compliance purposes, then deleted
Server access and diagnostic logs referencing your accountRetained for a limited period for security and abuse-prevention purposes, then deleted or anonymized
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Once processed, we cannot recover your transactions, wallets, budgets, or history.
