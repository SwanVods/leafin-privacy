1. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to all users of the Service, including the Leafin Android application, the Leafin progressive web application, and their associated backend infrastructure. It does not apply to any third-party website, application, or service that may be linked to or from the Service, including the third-party service providers described in Section 6, each of which maintains its own separate privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo (if available) from your Google account. If you use the Service in guest mode, no account information is collected and your data is not linked to any identity.
  • Financial information you record. Transaction amounts, dates, descriptions, categories, and associated notes; wallet names, types, and balances; budget amounts and periods; and custom category names and icons. All financial information is entered by you and reflects your own personal record-keeping — we do not connect to your bank accounts, cards, or any financial institution.
  • Receipt images. If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the photo or document you provide is transmitted to our AI service provider to extract transaction details (merchant, amount, date, line items). We do not retain a copy of the image after it has been processed.
  • Conversational input. Free-text transaction entries (e.g., "coffee 35k") and messages you send to the in-app AI assistant are processed to extract structured data and generate responses or financial insights.
  • Customer support communications. If you contact us directly (e.g., by email), we retain the content of that communication and any information you choose to provide in order to respond to you.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Local application data. The Service stores your data locally on your device in an on-device database as its primary copy, which is then synchronized with our servers.
  • Server and diagnostic logs. Our backend servers automatically record standard request metadata for security, debugging, and reliability purposes, which may include IP address, timestamp, requested endpoint, device/app version, and error information. We do not use this data for advertising or behavioral profiling.
  • Push notification identifiers. If you enable push notifications, we store the device- or browser-issued subscription token required to deliver notifications to you.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, Google shares certain profile information with us as described in Section 2.1, subject to the permissions you grant during that sign-in flow and Google's own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account and synchronize your data across sessions and devices;
  • To display your transactions, wallets, budgets, and spending analytics within the Service;
  • To generate AI-powered financial insights, categorize transactions, and process receipt scans and conversational input;
  • To send transactional and account-related notifications, such as budget threshold alerts or low-balance warnings;
  • To process subscription purchases, renewals, and trials, and to verify purchase and entitlement status;
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper technical functioning of the Service, including diagnosing and preventing fraud, abuse, or technical issues;
  • To enforce applicable usage limits associated with free and paid subscription tiers; and
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from public authorities.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party, and we do not use your financial data to serve you advertising.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal data (including Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection), we process your information on the following bases: (a) performance of the contract between you and us to provide the Service you have requested; (b) your consent, such as when you opt in to push notifications or grant permission during Google Sign-In; (c) our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service, provided those interests are not overridden by your data protection interests; and (d) compliance with legal obligations to which we are subject.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We disclose information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. With third-party vendors who process information on our behalf as described in Section 6, strictly for the purposes of providing their respective services to us.
  • Legal compliance and protection of rights. Where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Leafin, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, in which case information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality safeguards.
  • With your consent. For any other purpose disclosed to you at the time information is collected and to which you consent.

7. Data Storage, Security & International Transfers

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit using HTTPS/TLS, using token-based authentication with limited validity periods rather than storing passwords, and enforcing per-account access controls on our backend. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where our infrastructure and service providers are located, your information may be processed or stored in a country other than the one in which you reside. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure that such transfers are conducted in a manner consistent with applicable data protection law.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service to you. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your associated transactions, wallets, budgets, categories, and notification history from our servers, except where retention is required to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements. Receipt images are not retained beyond the time necessary to process them. Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes and are then deleted or anonymized in the ordinary course of business.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Access and portability — you may export your transaction data (available as a Premium feature within the Service);
  • Correction — you may edit or correct your transaction, wallet, budget, and category data at any time directly within the Service;
  • Deletion — you may permanently delete your account and associated data at any time via Help → Delete My Data within the Service; this action is irreversible;
  • Withdrawal of consent — you may disable push notifications or revoke Google Sign-In access through your device or Google account settings at any time;
  • Objection and restriction — where applicable law provides such rights, you may contact us using the details in Section 13 to exercise them.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the time required by applicable law.

10. Advertising and Tracking

Leafin does not display third-party advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) for ad targeting, and does not integrate third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDKs. Any advertising-related permission declared by the application is provided solely to comply with platform requirements and is not used to serve or personalize advertising.

11. Children's Privacy

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child in violation of this Policy, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information promptly.
